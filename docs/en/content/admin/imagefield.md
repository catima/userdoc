**Images in high fidelity formats (TIFF and HEIC)**

Catima allows the upload of files in TIFF and HEIC formats; however, as browsers cannot display such images directly, during the upload of such files in an image field, the file is converted and saved in JPG format. 

> **Nota Bene**: if you need to keep a copy of TIFF or HEIC files in their original format in Catima, we suggest that you save them in a field of type 'file'.
