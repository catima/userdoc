**Invite a user who does not yet have a Catima account**

In the "Users and Groups" tab of the *Set up* mode, click on "New user".

Enter the email address of the user to invite.

![](assets/setup/invitenewuser.png)

Choose the preferred language of the user and the role assigned to the user.

> **Note**: a visual symbol indicates the roles that are irrelevant (for instance in the above example, the roles "User" or "Member" will provide no access, because the catalog is restricted to the staff of the catalog).

Click on "Send Invitation", the user will receive an invitation via email.
