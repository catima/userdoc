The simple search is based on a word or several words. It will perfomr a search on the entire catalog content and look for the keywords in all fields of the catalog.

This search provides two options:
- By default the **Broad** mode is activated. 
- The  **Strict** mode can be selected in order to exclude results that are potentially less relevant.

![](assets/search/simple_search_broad_strict.png)

**Default simple search - 'Broad' mode**

In the text bar, enter a search word (for instance "Rowling" or "Swiss") and click on the "*Search*" button. 

A list of cliquable results are displayed and allow to access the chosen content or change the keyword and do another search.

**Alternative simple search - 'Strict' mode**

This mode is valuable for instance when you obtain too many results or results that are apparently less relevant for you. This mode allows to do a more narrow search excluding those potentially irrelevant results of the broad search.

It may happen that, after activating this mode and trying a few different words, you still do not obtain the results you are looking for. In that case, you could try the <a href="#advanced-search">advanced search</a> which provide options to restrict the search of specific words to specific fields.
